From clerk to technician to developer

I found IT while working as a clerk at Malaysia Building Society Berhad, watching a friend who was an IT technician. I didn't know then how wide the field was, so I studied computer software applications at Kolej Komuniti Segamat 2 and built my first system, a room booking system in PHP, during my internship.

I then spent three and a half years as an on-site IT technician in Segamat, fixing PCs, laying network cable and installing CCTV and door access for homes, shops, factories, schools and government offices.

In 2022 I joined HeveaBoard Berhad as IT Officer. In 2024 I took over the corporate website from an external vendor, and since then most of my time has gone into building and running the company's web systems for medical claims, container inspection and diesel operations.

I'm now looking for a dedicated web or software developer role where I can keep building systems people rely on, and grow as part of a development team.

Based in Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia. Malay (native), English (professional working proficiency).
